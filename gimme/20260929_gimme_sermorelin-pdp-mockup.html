What is Sermorelin?

Sermorelin is a synthetic peptide that mimics the growth hormone releasing hormone (GHRH), a signal the body naturally uses to stimulate the release of growth hormone from the pituitary gland. In medical settings, Sermorelin may be prescribed by a licensed physician as part of a treatment plan designed to support growth hormone signaling.

What is Sermorelin treatment used for?

Licensed medical providers may prescribe Sermorelin in certain cases where supporting natural growth hormone signaling is appropriate. Your provider will evaluate your health history, symptoms, and goals before determining whether Sermorelin could be included in your care plan.

How does Sermorelin work?

Sermorelin works by signaling the pituitary gland to release growth hormone naturally. Unlike growth hormone replacement therapy, which provides external growth hormone, Sermorelin encourages the body's own regulatory system to produce growth hormone through normal physiological pathways. A physician determines whether this approach may be appropriate based on your medical history and treatment goals.

What are the potential side effects?

Possible side effects of sermorelin include redness, swelling, pain, or itching at the injection site, as well as headache, flushing, dizziness, nausea, or temporary changes in taste. Contact your provider if side effects persist, worsen, or become difficult to manage. Seek urgent medical care for trouble breathing, swelling of the face or throat, widespread hives, chest tightness, fainting, or other symptoms of a serious allergic reaction. This is not a complete list of possible side effects.

What is Sermorelin?New

Your body still makes growth hormone. It just makes less.

After 30, human growth hormone (HGH) levels decline by up to 15% per decade.1 Lower HGH is linked to changes in muscle, sleep, recovery, and metabolism.6

Sermorelin is a peptide that mimics growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). Instead of replacing HGH, it signals your pituitary gland to release more of your own.7
