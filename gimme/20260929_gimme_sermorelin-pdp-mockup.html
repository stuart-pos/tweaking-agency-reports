What is Sermorelin?New

Your body still makes growth hormone. It just makes less.

After 30, human growth hormone (HGH) levels decline by up to 15% per decade.1 Lower HGH is linked to changes in muscle, sleep, recovery, and metabolism.6

Sermorelin is a peptide that mimics growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). Instead of replacing HGH, it signals your pituitary gland to release more of your own.7

The science of SermorelinNew

Works with your body, not over it

Sermorelin is a GHRH analog. Synthetic HGH replaces growth hormone directly. Sermorelin prompts your body to make its own, in natural pulses.1,7
