Why this matters for Gimme

Shopping is Gimme’s #2 campaign. Showing a $x/month × 3 SKU in the feed has been a challenge — Gimme LPs historically hero the full 3-month total (e.g. $469 tirz). Embody keeps the monthly number as the only large price from ad → PDP → plan card, and only reveals the prepaid total in the last block of checkout.

Target CPA is $400. If Gimme can match this display pattern, Shopping cards can compete on $163/mo (tirz) / $130/mo (sema) without putting $489 / $390 in the merchant price field.

What Gimme should copy

  1. Keep $163/mo (tirz) / $130/mo (sema) as the only large price on Shopping LPs and 3-month intake cards. Do not hero $489 / $390 there.
  2. Put billed-today in small type on the card and in the order summary only — Embody’s pattern: “$126/mo flat forever” on the button, $378 on Total.
  3. Do not put the 3-month total in the Shopping feed price field if the goal is a $x/mo card. Embody’s feed/PDP is monthly; checkout does the prepaid math.
  4. Legal wrapper can still be a subscription (Gimme already is). Embody discloses auto-renew at lead capture, then talks “flat forever / cancel anytime” on checkout.

Watch the bait: treatment cards say tirz “$119/month” (12-month teaser) while Shopping/PDP/3-month card say $126/mo and checkout charges $378. Default duration before we clicked was monthly at $129 due today — not the 3-month SKU from the ad.
