Why this matters: Under FTC advertising rules, price claims must be truthful and not misleading. Absolute “for life / every month / no hike / no surprise” claims are contradicted by a large cluster of consumer reports describing month-2 jumps to $299 and dose-tied price increases. Countdown timers + “Was $199 / Now $89” create urgency around a price that many consumers say does not hold.

5. Pattern synthesis (for NAD / FTC tip)

  1. Net impression of ads/LPs: locked monthly price, no hike, no surprises, often “for life.”
  2. Consumer experience cluster: first month at promo (~$89–$149), second month ~$299 or other higher amount, often without meaningful notice or consent to the new price.
  3. Refund friction: policy makes most charges non-refundable after intake; consumers report stonewalling.
  4. Alias / entity confusion: CareValidate / Care GLP / CASA PHARMA appear in consumer filings alongside AltRx.
  5. Platform integrity: Trustpilot removed fake reviews and hid the score — consistent with reputation manipulation concerns.
