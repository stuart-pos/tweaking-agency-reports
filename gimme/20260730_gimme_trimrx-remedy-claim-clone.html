TrimRx efficacy claims = Remedy Meds copy (not independent self-reported data)
Client: Gimme Care · Captured 2026-07-30 · Add-on to FTC tip pack

← All Gimme reports · FTC tip evidence pack · Filing runbook

Finding: TrimRx’s “94.6% success rate” and “lose weight twice as fast as the industry average within 90 days” claims on offers.trimrx.com/offer-v2 are not independent TrimRx clinical results. They are brand-swapped lifts of Remedy Meds marketing copy that is still live on Remedy campaign pages today — including the same “self-reported… approximately 300,000… intake questionnaire and every 3–4 weeks thereafter” methodology boilerplate. TrimRx’s offer-v2 also still serves the older Remedy affiliate LP shell (“America’s #1 Most Trusted,” “We Outperform,” vial-box hero, Weight Loss Warranty seal) that Google continues to associate with Remedy campaign URLs even after Remedy redesigned its public hero. The stats travel with the cloned template — they are not TrimRx-measured data.
Where the “old Remedy LP” is: Live Remedy campaign URLs such as remedymeds.com/?rm_campaign=first100 and ?rm_campaign=BWLM no longer render the dark-blue “America’s #1 Most Trusted” hero (redesigned to “willpower problem”), but they still publish the identical efficacy block (2x / 94.6% / ~300k self-report). Google’s index still titles those same campaign URLs with the old template (“America’s #1 Most Trusted GLP-1 Program” / “clinically meaningful weight loss outcomes” / “refund you every penny”). TrimRx frozen that older shell on offer-v2 and kept the numbers.

Why this is not real TrimRx self-reported data:
  1. Exact same success rate to one decimal place (94.6%) as Remedy.
  2. Exact same comparative claim (“twice as fast as the industry average within 90 days”).
  3. Near-identical methodology paragraph, including the same ~300,000 cohort size Remedy uses for Remedy members — re-labeled as TrimRx customers.
  4. Same LP shell TrimRx still runs (“America’s #1 / We Outperform / Weight Loss Warranty”) matches the old Remedy affiliate template Google still associates with Remedy campaign URLs.
That is template plagiarism with a brand find-replace — not competent and reliable scientific evidence that TrimRx measured its own cohort.

4. Paste-ready addendum for FTC / CA AG narrative

ADDENDUM — EFFICACY CLAIMS COPIED FROM REMEDY MEDS TrimRx’s 94.6% / “twice as fast as the industry average within 90 days” claims on offers.trimrx.com/offer-v2 are not independent TrimRx clinical results. As of 2026-07-30, Remedy Meds still publishes the same comparative claim and the same 94.6% figure, with nearly identical “self-reported data from approximately 300,000 … intake questionnaire … every 3-4 weeks” methodology language (e.g. https://remedymeds.com/?rm_campaign=GEO100). TrimRx’s version swaps in “TrimRx” (and sometimes 66,260 / 300,000) but keeps the template. This indicates the efficacy superiority advertising is cloned marketing copy, not brand-substantiated evidence under FTC advertising substantiation standards for health and comparative claims.
