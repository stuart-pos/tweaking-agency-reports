2. FTC form draft — paste into ReportFraud.ftc.gov

Select all in the box → copy → paste into the FTC “what happened” field. Fill brackets with your name/agency.

COMPETITOR TIP — MetaFit Pharma Solutions LLC d/b/a TrimRx (San Diego, CA) I work at a marketing agency for competing telehealth weight-loss brands. I am reporting TrimRx for deceptive advertising and unfair subscription practices that appear to violate FTC Act Section 5 and ROSCA. I did not personally purchase from them; this is a public-interest tip based on live ads/pages and widespread consumer complaints. COMPANY MetaFit Pharma Solutions LLC d/b/a TrimRx Websites: https://trimrx.com · https://offers.trimrx.com · https://start.trimrx.com Phone: (888) 896-1612 · Email: hello@trimrx.org Address: 12636 High Bluff Drive Ste 400, San Diego, CA 92130 PROBLEM 1 — UNSUBSTANTIATED EFFICACY / SUPERIORITY CLAIMS On https://offers.trimrx.com/offer-v2 (live as of 2026-07-30), TrimRx claims: • “94.6% success rate” • Members lose weight “2x faster” / “twice as fast as the industry average within 90 days” • “America’s #1 Most Trusted GLP-1 Program” and similar #1 rankings These are presented as proven clinical/industry superiority. Footnotes attribute results to self-reported customer data (approx. 66,260–300,000 members), not controlled clinical trials vs competitors. FTC substantiation rules for health and comparative claims require competent and reliable scientific evidence before dissemination. Net impression: superior, proven medical outcomes for compounded GLP-1s (same page admits compounded meds are not FDA-approved). Evidence: Exhibits 01, 02, 05 (screenshots captured 2026-07-30). PROBLEM 2 — DECEPTIVE WEIGHT-LOSS / MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE Same LP markets a “5-Month Weight-Loss Guarantee,” “Weight Loss Warranty,” and money-back-if-you-don’t-hit-goal language (“refund you every penny”). https://trimrx.com/terms-and-conditions limits refunds to (1) medical disqualification or (2) billing error; states orders are non-refundable once intake is submitted; cancellation after a charge does not refund prior charges. Advertising vs contract mismatch is deceptive. Evidence: Exhibits 01, 03 vs 08, 09. PROBLEM 3 — BAIT PRICING + HARD CANCEL / NEGATIVE OPTION (ROSCA) Ads/offer pages promote low monthly prices and “No Hidden Fees” while Terms list higher plan prices (e.g. GLP-1 $199/$299; GLP-1+GIP $349/$399). Public Trustpilot (~3.6/5, 5,400+ reviews) and BBB complaints: advertised price ≠ charged price; inability to cancel; continued charges after cancel requests; non-response to refill forms treated as renew consent. Example public complaints: • https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a63c641143886095dffa2c1 ($144 → ~$232) • https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a63a9a61a0dca562f268391 (cannot cancel; silence = renew) • https://www.bbb.org/scamtracker/lookupscam/1254827 (2 months charged; Everflow affiliate ID in URL) • https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a62f4d535512c64a1cea0ae • https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a62753472539de53907baeb Evidence: Exhibits 04, 10, 12. PROBLEM 4 — AFFILIATE DISTRIBUTION Multiple Google Ads advertisers and Everflow affiliate tracking amplify these claims beyond one corporate ad account. REQUEST Please enter into Consumer Sentinel and consider investigation for deceptive health/efficacy claims, deceptive guarantees, and ROSCA/negative-option violations.
