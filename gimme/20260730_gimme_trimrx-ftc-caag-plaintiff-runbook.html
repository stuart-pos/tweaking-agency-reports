Do I need my name / title / agency on the FTC tip?
No — not required. ReportFraud.ftc.gov lets you submit anonymously. How much personal info you give is up to you.

Recommended minimum: leave an email so you get a confirmation number emailed back (FTC form also cannot attach screenshots — so confirmation + this evidence pack URL is your paper trail).

Optional but useful: your name if you’re fine being contacted for follow-up. Title/agency are not required fields — only include them in the narrative if you want to signal this is a competitor tip (helps framing; not mandatory).

Tradeoff: FTC may share reporter info with other enforcers (and sometimes the company) under its Privacy Act routine uses. If you want distance, stay anonymous + email-only, or use a work email without title/agency in the body.
CA AG heads-up: Their form asks you to authorize sending the complaint to TrimRx — MetaFit will see it. Use your real contact there (form requires it). Don’t put anything you wouldn’t want them to read.
