Hero leads “Starting at $145/mo*” with footnote: 12-month plan + first-time promo. Brand cash prices cited by VA: Ozempic/Mounjaro ~$1,300; Wegovy ~$1,600; Zepbound ~$1,400. No membership fee; shipping included.

What we liked

  • Clear 1/3/6/12 plan grid with first vs ongoing shown
  • No membership fee; flat rate within a plan (not by dose)
  • Compounded + branded on one platform
  • Free expedited shipping; VA cold-chain strong (ice packs frozen)
  • LegitScript + phone support (800) 597-0884
  • Easy cancel in VA hands-on (~2 min)
  • Large Trustpilot volume vs peers
  • Same-day async approvals reported

Where it falls short

  • Ad “starting at $X” = first month / long bundle, not forever MTM
  • Pharmacies not listed on site
  • Public complaints largely unanswered since ~Aug 2025 (VA)
  • Chat leads with AI (“Taylor”)
  • No coaching / lifestyle program
  • ID upload or last-4 SSN at intake
  • No insurance even for brands
  • LA exclusion conflict (site vs reviews)

Support & reputation tone

  • Channels: Phone Mon–Fri ~9–6 EST (VA); email hello@effecty.com; portal messaging; AI chat first
  • Trustpilot: ~4.5–4.6 / ~700+ · reply rate to negatives low (~12%); typical reply lag >1 month
  • Tone: Mostly silent / non-engaging on public complaint threads after Aug 2025 — VA’s “Ignored Complaints” hook
  • BBB: B+ · ~2 complaints (secondary; re-verify)
  • Reddit: Mixed / thin vs Trustpilot volume
  • Pharmacy (VA asked support): Emerald, VialsRx, Greenwich, GoGo Meds, Pharmacy Hub — not on website

For you if / Not for you if

  • For you if: You want brands + compounded; you will pay ongoing plan rate after promo; you value overnight cold chain.
  • Not for you if: You need pharmacy names before you buy; you expect public complaint replies; you assumed $95/$145 is your permanent monthly.

What we liked (aligns with VA)

  • No coaching / nutrition / community

  • Cancel option buried (delay/pause easier to find)
  • “Licensed US pharmacies” claim unverifiable without disclosure

Support & reputation tone — critical for listicle

  • Hours (VA): Human support M–F ~8am–6pm EST; limited weekends; AI chat off-hours routes to email
  • Trustpilot: ~4.2–4.3 · hundreds of reviews · reviews generally sound real
  • Reply example (VA): Clinic publicly argued a patient had discarded medication and “assumed” shipment contents rather than leading with a solution — framed as defensive and oversharing patient-specific detail
  • Reddit (r/gimmecare + aggregators): Mixed — price/results vs dosing inconsistency, CS friction, side-effect worry · NEEDS HANDS-ON fresh scrape (403 this run)
  • Pharmacy: VA package labeled Hallandale Pharmacy (FL Clear license; FDA Warning Letter 2022 + Form 483 June 2025 noted by VA)
  • Cold chain (VA): Ice packs present but damp/melted; cold chain “not at full strength” on arrival

For you if / Not for you if

  • For you if: You want predictable all-in flat rates, async care, speed, and HSA/FSA paperwork.
  • Not for you if: You need pharmacy name before ordering, live phone support, or a clean public complaint-handling record.

  • Tirz MTM expensive vs Mondae/Gimme entry
  • “Same price every dose” fine-print caveats
  • Coaching claim vs medication-only reality conflict
  • Full-body selfie / weight verification awkward
  • Generic / copy-paste Trustpilot replies

  • BBB: Main reputation landmine for listicle honesty
  • Reddit: Split (price/speed vs CS/billing) · NEEDS HANDS-ON fresh sampling

For you if / Not for you if

  • For you if: You want straightforward sema bundles and don’t mind cash-pay without pharmacy choice UI.
  • Not for you if: BBB complaint history matters, or you need reliable phone support.

9. Listicle writing kit

Copy-ready blocks for null.health ranking pages. Prefer VA tone: specific, slightly skeptical, cite Trustpilot/Reddit patterns, call out ad vs checkout math.

  • Price line: “Hero ‘from $145/mo’ is the 12-month ongoing rate after a $45 first month — month-to-month compounded GLP-1 is $195 after a $95 first month.”
  • Support gotcha: “Phone exists, but chat starts with AI; public Trustpilot complaints often go unanswered.”
  • Pharmacy: “Site says licensed partners; reviewers who asked were told Emerald / VialsRx / Greenwich / GoGo / Pharmacy Hub.”

Ozari Health — one-liners

  • Price line: “$86/mo sema and $125/mo tirz are first-time 3-month starter rates; quarterly is $145 / $199 and monthly $175 / $235.”
  • Pharmacy win: “Unlike most peers, Ozari publishes Hallandale Pharmacy and VialsRx.”
  • Caveat: “Brand is brand-new — don’t fabricate a review consensus that doesn’t exist yet.”

Coby Health — one-liners

  • Price line: “Starts at $159 for 0.25 mg sema, then $199 → $229 → $249 as you climb — bundles only soften the curve.”
  • Support win: “Human phone and email; no AI chat gate.”
  • Pharmacy: “Not listed on site; third-party orders cite GreenwichRX / Hallandale.”

Gimme Care — one-liners (use VA specifics)

  • Price line: “Site: $160 sema / $199 tirz month-to-month, or $130 / $163 on 3-month prepay. Ads often lead with the prepay number.”
  • Support gotcha: “No phone. AI chat after hours. Trustpilot replies that defend with patient-case details (discarded meds / ‘assumed’ contents) read poorly.”

Amble — one-liners

Mondae — one-liners
