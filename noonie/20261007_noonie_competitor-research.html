Midi

Insurance-covered virtual practice

Available in all 50 states. Revenue is the visit, not a medication subscription. No membership. Labs and prescriptions go to outside labs and local pharmacies. Self-pay is $250 for the first visit and $150 for follow-ups.

Prices

  • Self-pay $250 first visit / $150 follow-up
  • Compounded GLP-1 from $164 per 4 weeks, shipping included
  • Microdosing advertised from $34/week
  • Brand GLP-1 sent to the patient’s pharmacy

Why they win

  • Specialist care, not a cash medication store
  • Midlife women’s health clinicians, local pharmacy, FDA-approved HRT
  • 25,000+ patients a week, insurance reach above 45 million women, more than $250M raised, valued above $1B in February 2026

Praise. Felt heard after being dismissed. Fast HRT, insurance, pharmacy convenience.

Where it breaks. Provider quality varies. Billing surprises. Hard to reschedule. Some visits feel scripted.

Channels. Search, Meta, SEO, and employer or payer deals. Semrush estimated 692K organic visits and 6.51K paid-search visits in August 2026. A 2026 national campaign with Amy Schumer ran across TV, radio, out-of-home, and social.

Winona

Cash-pay menopause subscription, ages 35–59

Care, messaging, and shipping sit inside the medication price. Owns two 503A compounding pharmacies. Does not prescribe testosterone, GLP-1, or pellets.

Prices

  • Progesterone from $39/month
  • Estrogen tablets from $54/month
  • Estrogen cream, combo cream, and vaginal cream from $89/month
  • Estrogen patch from $149/month
  • DHEA $27 per three-month supply

Why they win

  • Questionnaire, review, ship. No required labs.
  • Care is included in the drug price
  • Owned pharmacy, compounded formulas, and a 23K+ Facebook group

Praise. Fast start, responsive messages, symptom relief, no separate doctor bill.

Where it breaks. Compounded-drug trust. Cash can cost more than insured retail. Cancellation, renewals, and lost shipments. Care can feel transactional.

Channels. Meta creates demand, Google captures it, email and SMS recover checkout. About 2,887 active Meta ads. Semrush: 5.07K paid-search visits and 29.6K organic visits in August 2026.

Joi

Diagnostics-first cash platform

Labs, then a consult, then a care plan. Makes money on labs, a $50/month care fee billed quarterly, medication, supplements, and GLP-1. Shares the backend with Blokes. Medications are on top of the care fee.

Prices

  • Care $50/month, billed quarterly
  • October 7 promo panels: 56 markers $74, 71 markers $140, 110 markers $245. Recheck at checkout.
  • Testosterone $59 injection or $69 cream
  • Estrogen $49 capsule, $69 cream, or $129 patch
  • Progesterone $54 capsule or $69 cream

Why they win

  • Labs first, then a plan
  • Live clinician visits, follow-up labs, and coaching
  • A wide catalog raises lifetime value

Praise. Thorough panels. Felt heard. Access to therapies local care would not offer.

Where it breaks. Total cost gets messy. Delays, support turnover, pharmacy confusion, surprise fees, and aggressive cross-sell. The FDA issued a warning letter in June 2026 on compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide claims.

Channels. Meta, Google, affiliates, podcasts, and CTV. The public Google footprint is much smaller than Midi or Winona and sits under the Blokes advertiser.

Blokes

Men’s version of Joi

TRT, performance, longevity, and weight. Care, labs, coaching, and community sit inside the TRT plan.

Prices

  • 3 months: $149/month, $447 upfront
  • 6 months: $129/month, $774 upfront
  • 12 months: $99/month, $1,188 upfront
  • Hair and ED add-ons advertised at $1/month for active members

Where it breaks. Price and long commitments. Support and fulfillment complaints. Ratings are mixed with Joi, so they are less diagnostic.

Channels. Podcasts, creators, affiliates, and CTV. 120 tracked YouTube videos, 106 of them on one show. Not a search leader.

Where Noonie can fit

A usable position. Clear hormone care for women who are done being dismissed: a real clinician visit, a written plan, a transparent total, and a person they can reach. The narrower wedge is symptom-led HRT with testosterone and no routine panel, between Winona’s no-lab simplicity and Joi’s lab-first model. Keep GLP-1 off the homepage pitch. Winona does not prescribe testosterone or GLP-1. That opening only works after Noonie says who it is for and what $99 includes.

The current site is fast and shows $99/month, FSA/HSA, free shipping, and no required hormone testing. It does not yet give a buyer a reason to pick Noonie over brands with more proof and a clearer care model.

Evidence limits

  • Prices come from official pages on October 7, 2026. Promo prices should be rechecked at checkout.
  • Semrush visits and social counts are directional, not invoices.
  • Google Ads Transparency shows creatives, not spend.
  • Winona’s $1.5M/month and 3.2x ROAS are agency-reported across channels, not Google-only, and not audited.
  • Reviews describe failure modes. They are not a measure of typical clinical quality.
