# GLP-1 cheaper-than-Mondae competitive landscape

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Client:** Mondae  
**Scope:** DTC telehealth advertisers selling compounded semaglutide and/or tirzepatide — **Embody included** as a full competitor and in cheaper tallies when under Mondae.

---

## 1. Executive summary

Mondae’s 1-month cash-pay bars: **semaglutide $87/mo**, **tirzepatide $126/mo**.

| Question | Count | Brands |
|----------|------:|--------|
| **Cheaper on semaglutide** (all-in 1-mo entry **&lt; $87**) | **2** | SnagRx ($69), Embody ($79) |
| **Cheaper on tirzepatide** (all-in 1-mo entry **&lt; $126**) | **1** | SnagRx ($119) |
| **Cheaper on either molecule** | **2** | SnagRx, Embody |

**Embody:** cheaper on **sema only** ($79 vs $87). Tirz at **$129** is **$3 above** Mondae — not cheaper.

**Near-miss / auction noise (not in primary cheaper count):**
- **AltRx** sema **$89** — $2 above Mondae (not cheaper; often looks like a peer in Shopping)
- **BrightMeds** tirz **$119/mo** on 3-mo promo (ads lean on this); 1-mo MTM is **$149** — not cheaper on the 1-mo rule
- **Mochi** ads **$60 / $90** med-only; all-in ongoing ≈ **$139 / $169** with $79 membership — **not** cheaper all-in; first-month all-in ≈ **$99 / $129** (sema still above $87; tirz $3 above $126)

**Takeaway:** On a true 1-month all-in basis, only **two** advertisers undercut Mondae (Embody on sema; SnagRx on both). The auction still fills with **headline** undercuts (Mochi, BrightMeds promo, IVIM “from $75”) that are not Mondae-comparable all-in prices.

## 2. Mondae price bar & rules

| | Semaglutide | Tirzepatide |
|--|------------:|------------:|
| **1-mo entry (comparison anchor)** | **$87/mo** | **$126/mo** |
| 3-mo effective (footnote only) | $75/mo | $124/mo |

**Cheaper =** advertised compounded **cash-pay** entry **$/mo** for that molecule, **strictly below** the Mondae 1-mo anchor.

**All-in preference:** When a brand charges a required membership on top of medication (e.g. Mochi, IVIM), the primary table uses **ongoing all-in** (entry med + standard membership). Med-only / promo floors are noted so auction claims are visible without polluting the cheaper tally.

**In universe:** DTC telehealth with paid presence selling compounded sema and/or tirz — **including Embody (`joinem.co`)**.

**Out of universe:** Mondae / Gimme / Amble; brand-name-only retail with no compounded cash path; insurance / $25-coupon framing; pure aggregators with no DTC sale.

**Advertiser?:** Yes if seen in Shopping SERP captures, Search ads, or known paid transparency in this workspace / live SERP (2026-09).

## 6. Implications (vs $250 CPA / transparent pricing)

- **Price position is strong on 1-mo all-in.** Almost nobody undercuts Mondae on both molecules; Embody only wins sema by $8; SnagRx is the rare dual undercutter but is not a Large/Mid scale peer.
- **Auction copy ≠ checkout reality.** Mochi / BrightMeds / IVIM can still steal clicks with lower *headlines*. Mondae’s win theme (**transparent pricing**) should punch **all-in monthly** (no membership, no bait dose) rather than race SnagRx’s $69 promo.
- **Tirz is Mondae’s cleaner price story vs Embody** ($126 vs $129) while sema is where Embody presses. Align Search/Shopping creative weight: defend sema with value/speed; lean tirz on price parity + dual-agonist.
- **AltRx $89** is the Shopping “almost” — expect constant impression overlap; don’t treat +$2 as a lost price war.
- **CPA $250 target:** cheap-headline competitors may inflate CPC / lower CVR if landing pages reveal membership stacks. Prefer RSA/extensions that force the all-in comparison early (reduces junk click-through that never converts).
