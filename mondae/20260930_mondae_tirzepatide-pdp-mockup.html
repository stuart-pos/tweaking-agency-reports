What is Mondae?

Mondae is a telehealth platform that connects eligible patients with licensed healthcare providers for evaluation and, when medically appropriate, prescription treatment. If prescribed, medications are dispensed and shipped by a licensed pharmacy, with ongoing access to your healthcare provider throughout your treatment.

How does Mondae work?

Begin by completing a secure online intake questionnaire detailing your health history, current medications, and treatment goals. A U.S.-licensed healthcare provider will review your information and conduct a medical consultation to determine whether treatment is appropriate for you. If prescribed, your medication will be shipped directly to your door with tracking information. Throughout your treatment, you can securely message your healthcare provider through your online patient portal.

Do I need insurance?

No, insurance is not required.

What is compounded tirzepatide?New

A once-weekly injection. A provider decides if it fits.

Compounded tirzepatide is made for one patient when a licensed provider prescribes it. Tirzepatide acts on two hormone receptors, GLP-1 and GIP.

FDA-approved medicines that contain tirzepatide are sold as Mounjaro and Zepbound. The compounded medication on this page is not approved by the FDA. It is not a generic, and it is not a substitute for those products.
