Frequently asked questions

Why is Mondae so much cheaper than other options?

Mondae keeps pricing simple: one flat monthly price that covers your medication, your online medical visit, and ongoing provider support, with no membership fee on top. Compounded medications are prepared by licensed U.S. pharmacies and are only prescribed when a U.S.-licensed provider decides they’re appropriate for you.

What exactly do I pay?

Compounded semaglutide is $87/month month-to-month, or $75/month on the 3-month plan ($225 billed every 3 months). Compounded tirzepatide is $126 for a single month, or $124/month when you buy 3 months ($372 billed once). That price includes your medication at any dose, your online visit, provider messaging, and shipping. No membership fees, no hidden costs.

What’s the difference between a single month and 3 months?

A single month lets you pay as you go. Three months lowers your monthly price: $75/month for semaglutide or $124/month for tirzepatide. Either way, every dose is the same price and there are no membership or hidden fees. You can split a 3-month payment with Klarna, Afterpay, or Affirm at checkout.

Is compounded medication legitimate?

Compounded medications are prepared by state-licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies for individual patients, based on a provider’s medical judgment. They are not approved by the FDA, and the FDA does not review compounded medications for safety, effectiveness, or quality before they are dispensed. Treatment is only provided when a licensed provider decides it’s appropriate for you.

How do I get started?

Complete a short online health intake. A U.S.-licensed provider reviews your information, and if treatment is appropriate, your medication ships discreetly to your door. If you’re not prescribed, you’re not charged.

Do I need insurance or a prior prescription?

No insurance is required, and you don’t need an existing prescription. The online visit handles the prescription if a provider decides treatment is right for you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your semaglutide plan anytime from your Mondae account. Tirzepatide is a one-time payment for the supply you choose.

What states is Mondae available in?

Mondae is available in all 50 states.
