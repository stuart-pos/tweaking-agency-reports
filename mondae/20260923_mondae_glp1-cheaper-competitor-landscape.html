6. Implications (vs $250 CPA / transparent pricing)

  • Price position is strong on 1-mo all-in. Almost nobody undercuts Mondae on both molecules; Embody only wins sema by $8; SnagRx is the rare dual undercutter but not a Large/Mid scale peer.
  • Auction copy ≠ checkout reality. Mochi / BrightMeds / IVIM can still steal clicks with lower headlines. Lean transparent-pricing creative on all-in monthly (no membership, no bait dose).
  • Tirz is Mondae’s cleaner price story vs Embody ($126 vs $129); sema is where Embody presses. Defend sema with value/speed; lean tirz on price parity + dual-agonist.
  • AltRx $89 is the Shopping “almost” — expect constant impression overlap; don’t treat +$2 as a lost price war.
  • CPA $250 target: cheap-headline competitors may inflate CPC / lower CVR when LPs reveal membership stacks. Force the all-in comparison early in RSA/extensions.
